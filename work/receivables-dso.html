Receivables & DSO

Open invoices bucketed by age, with days sales outstanding recomputed as cash comes in.

Try the model
TypeReceivables
FromFinance & Operations Analyst, Golden Quasar
SkillsAR aging · DSO · collections

Synthetic data Invented for this model. No client or employer information is used.

The measure

DSO is open receivables divided by credit sales for the period, times the days in the period.

Try it

Select an invoice to mark it collected. The aging buckets and the DSO figure update at once.

What to notice

The oldest invoices move DSO least per dollar but carry the most risk, which is why they are chased first.
