Test centre turnaround

Operations across four test centres delivering exam programs for CFA Institute, US CPA, securities licensing and medical licensure, each with its own client-mandated eligibility rules.

RoleOperations Consultant / Training Coordinator
WherePrometric Center · Yukon Consultants
WhenJan 2023 – May 2024
SkillsMulti-site operations · coaching · exception handling
68→94%Candidate survey satisfaction, four centres, about a year

15Team members coached
4Test centres

Situation

Centres trailing on the two numbers that decide a location’s future: candidate survey satisfaction at 68% and only 30% of available seats filled.

Action

Served as the escalation point for eligibility disputes and documentation discrepancies, verified identity and eligibility documentation at intake, and coached a 15-person team on documentation handling and exception judgment.

Result

Satisfaction up 26 points to 94% and utilization up 50 points to 80% within the year, with Regional Best Test Centre recognition.

About these figures. They are the results as I reported them during the role. I can no longer reproduce the measurement behind each one, so treat them as reported, not audited.
