The Cash Desk

A 13-week cash forecast you can drag. Change any week’s net flow and the position, the minimum and the buffer breaches recompute live.

Try the model
TypeCash forecasting
Built asA personal project
Skills13-week cash · stress testing · liquidity buffer

Synthetic data Invented for this model. No client or employer information is used.

What it shows

Thirteen weekly bars show net cash flow. The line above them is the running cash position against a minimum buffer.

Try it

Drag a bar, or tab to it and use the arrow keys. Press “Stress case” to slip receipts and land one large payment.

What to notice

The readout shows the lowest cash point, the week it happens, and how many weeks fall below the buffer.

How this was built. Built in November 2026 with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, at my direction. Claude wrote the code and the first draft of the text; I set the brief, reviewed the result against how the work is actually done, and decided what is published. It shows the reasoning I use at work. It is not evidence that I can program it unaided.
