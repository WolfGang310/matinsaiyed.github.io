Situation

A CAD $410K contract serving more than a million residents, measured weekly against contract targets.

Action

Issue resolution time cut 50% (5.0 to 2.5 days), monthly support tickets down 40%, report preparation down from 3 hours to 30 minutes. Delivered 125% of sales target with a $62.5K expansion.
