The three-way match

A cross-border contract is paid net of foreign withholding tax, so cash received never equals the invoice. Tie purchase order to invoice to cash, add the withholding back, and only the real breaks remain.

Try the model

FromFinance & Operations Analyst, Golden Quasar
SkillsThree-way match · withholding tax · variance tracing

Synthetic data Invented for this model. No client or employer information is used.

Step 1

Start with the toggle off. Every deposit shows a break, and none of them is real: the payer’s country withholds tax at source.

Step 2

Add the withholding back to each deposit. Four invoices now tie to the cent.

Step 3

Two genuine breaks remain. Select either red row to see whether it traces to the purchase order or to the payment.
